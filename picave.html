Know Your Notes.

Picave is the companion for banknote collectors in Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, Canada, the United States and the Eurozone. Identify notes, detect fancy serials, track your collection, and get notified when wishlist matches are found.

Features

Everything a Collector Needs

From serial number lookup to wishlist matching, Picave covers every aspect of the hobby.

Serial Number Identification

Instantly identify a banknote from six currencies by its serial number. Get denomination, series, signatures and issue date at a glance.

Fancy Serial Detection

Automatically detect radar, repeater, ladder, solid, and near-fancy serial patterns. The patterns that make a note truly special.

Condition Grading

Step-by-step guided grading helps you accurately assess your note's condition using standard numismatic criteria.

Collection Management

Build and track your personal collection with grades and notes. Everything synced securely so you never lose a record.

Valuation Data

Estimated values from collector-reported sales, plus what notes like yours are being listed for on eBay right now.

Community Matching

Add notes to your wishlist and get notified when another collector scans a match. Find the notes you need without the guesswork.
