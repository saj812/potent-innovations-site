Know Your Notes.

Picave is the companion for banknote collectors in Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, Canada, the United States and the Eurozone. Identify notes, detect fancy serials, track your collection, and hear from other collectors when they find a note on your wishlist.

Features

Everything a Collector Needs

From identifying a note to finding the ones you are missing.

Serial Number Identification

Identify a banknote from six currencies by its serial number and denomination: its year, series and signatures. Where one serial fits more than one note, the signatures are shown so you can pick yours.

Fancy Serial Detection

Detects radar, repeater, solid, ladder, binary and low serials, and near patterns one digit away. First and last prefixes, star notes and test notes are flagged too.

Condition Grading

Answer a short set of questions about your note's condition, with separate questions for paper and polymer, for a guide to its grade from Poor to Uncirculated. A guide, not an official grade.

Collection Management

Keep your notes with their grades, linked to your account and synced. Photos stay on your device and can be backed up to a file.

Valuation Data

With Pro, eBay sales of the same note from the last 90 days, with the price and date of each. Similar notes sold are shown as a guide, and current listings when nothing has sold.

Wishlist Matching

Add an exact serial, a pattern such as any radar, or a sequence of digits to your wishlist. Every note another Picave user looks up is checked against the wishlists for its currency, and when one matches yours, they can let you know it has been found, with its grade and a link to their eBay listing if they are selling it.
