Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Picave is a solo developer project that collects minimal user data to operate its banknote scanning and collection tracking features.

Data Collected

  • Account information: Email address and password, if you create an account via Supabase Auth.
  • App usage data: Banknote serial numbers you scan or enter, collection and wishlist entries, and subscription status through RevenueCat.
  • Submitted photos: A photo attached to a Submit Data request is uploaded with that submission so it can be reviewed.

Camera and Photos

When scanning, the camera image is read on your device to find the serial number and deleted straight away. Scanning images are never uploaded or stored.

Photos you add to a collection entry stay on your device. A photo you choose to attach to a Submit Data request is uploaded to online storage so the submission can be reviewed.

How Your Data Is Used

  • To enable serial number lookup
  • To track your collection entries
  • To send notifications about wishlisted items
  • To manage your subscription status

Your data is never sold, and is shared only with the service providers below, to run Picave.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase handles database and authentication.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions and in-app purchases. No payment card details are stored by Picave.
  • Resend delivers account emails.
  • Expo, with Firebase on Android and the Apple Push Notification service on iOS, delivers push notifications.
  • eBay receives the banknote denomination, year, grade and country to show current listings. No personal information is included.

Each service has its own privacy policy.

Data Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Settings, Delete Account. It is removed straight away, along with everything listed below.

You can also email picave@potentinnovations.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request. Include the email address associated with your account in the message body. Emailed requests are processed within 21 business days.

The following data will be deleted:

  • Account credentials
  • Saved collection
  • Wishlist
  • Any photos uploaded

Deleting your account does not cancel a Picave Pro subscription. Cancel it through the App Store or Google Play.

Anonymous, non-identifiable usage data may be retained for up to 30 days for security and fraud prevention purposes.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, reach out at picave@potentinnovations.com.
